tally

Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

tally is an app for logging your overtime. It's built so that your information stays yours: the developer never sees it, and nothing in the app tracks you.

What tally stores

The entries you log (dates, durations, start and end times, unpaid breaks, tags and hourly rates) and your settings (such as your name, goals, theme and currency). Everything is stored on your iPhone.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, tally keeps your entries and settings in sync across your devices using Apple's iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. This data is held by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer cannot access it.

What tally does not do

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • No data is sold or shared.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. tally receives only confirmation of what you've bought, never your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders and goal notifications are scheduled on your iPhone itself. Nothing is sent from a server.

Exports

When you export a PDF, CSV or Excel file, it's created on your iPhone and goes only where you choose to send it from the share sheet.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app. Deleting tally removes its data from your iPhone; to remove the copy in iCloud as well, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage on your iPhone.

Children

tally isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
