Help with tally

Need a hand, found a bug or have an idea? Email hellotallyapp@icloud.com and you'll get a reply, usually within a couple of days. It helps to mention your iPhone model and iOS version.

My entries aren't syncing to my other devices

Check you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device, and that iCloud is turned on for tally in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Changes can take a minute or two to appear.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open tally's Premium screen and tap restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought tally with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › tally, then tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I export my hours?

Use Export in tally to create a PDF, CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file, then send it anywhere from the share sheet. For opening in Excel, choose .xlsx so the columns are sized to fit.

Privacy

tally doesn't collect any of your data. Read the privacy policy.
